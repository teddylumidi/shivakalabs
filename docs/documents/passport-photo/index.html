Turn any clear photo into a passport-size photo with the background colour you choose. KSh 50, download as JPG or print-ready PDF.

Passport photo maker

  • Face the camera directly with a neutral expression and mouth closed
  • Both ears and your full face visible; hair off the forehead
  • No glasses, hats or headphones (religious head covers are usually accepted)
  • Even lighting with no shadows on your face
  • Kenya passports and visas need a white background

Your photo is processed on your device. It is uploaded only after payment, to create your files, and is not stored on our servers.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload a photo

    A clear, front-facing photo against a plain wall works best.

  2. 2

    Adjust and preview

    We remove the background and frame your face. Pick the size and colour.

  3. 3

    Pay KSh 50

    Pay with M-Pesa or card. Your photo is uploaded only after you pay.

  4. 4

    Download

    Get a JPG for online forms (under 300 KB) and a PDF sheet to print.
