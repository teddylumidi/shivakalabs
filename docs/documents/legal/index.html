Legal Documents

Affidavits, agreements, contracts and consents in standard Kenyan formats, ready to sign. Pay with M-Pesa or card, then download in Word or PDF.

AllCareerLettersLegalBusinessPersonalPassport Photo

Prepared from your details in standard Kenyan formats — not legal advice.

  • Affidavits only take effect once sworn and stamped before a Commissioner for Oaths.
  • Agreements should be signed by every party, with witnesses where shown.
  • For land, large sums or disputes, have an advocate review the document before signing.

KSh 200

Affidavit (Sworn Statement)

A general affidavit, e.g. name confirmation, loss of documents or marital status, ready to be sworn.

What you'll need
  • Your full name, ID number and address
  • What the affidavit is for
  • The facts you are swearing to

KSh 300

Tenancy / Lease Agreement

A residential or commercial tenancy agreement between landlord and tenant.

What you'll need
  • Landlord’s and tenant’s details (name, ID, address)
  • The premises and its location
  • Rent, deposit, start date and term
  • Any special terms

KSh 300

Motor Vehicle Sale Agreement

A sale agreement for a car, motorbike or truck, including NTSA transfer terms.

What you'll need
  • Seller’s and buyer’s details
  • Vehicle make, model, registration, chassis and engine numbers
  • Price and how it will be paid
  • Handover date

KSh 250

Sale Agreement (Goods / Assets)

For selling equipment, livestock, furniture, a business stock or other movable assets.

What you'll need
  • Seller’s and buyer’s details
  • What is being sold
  • Price, payment and delivery terms

KSh 250

Personal Loan Agreement

Record a loan between individuals or businesses: amount, repayment and default terms.

What you'll need
  • Lender’s and borrower’s details
  • Amount, interest and repayment schedule
  • Any security or guarantor

KSh 300

Employment Contract

A written contract of employment covering role, pay, hours, leave and termination.

What you'll need
  • Employer’s and employee’s details
  • Job title, duties and start date
  • Salary, hours, leave and notice period

KSh 300

Service / Freelance Contract

Agree scope, payment, timelines and ownership of work with a client or contractor.

What you'll need
  • Your and your client’s details
  • Scope of work and deliverables
  • Fees, payment schedule and deadlines

KSh 300

Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

Protect confidential information shared with a partner, employee or investor.

What you'll need
  • Both parties’ details
  • Why information is being shared
  • Mutual or one-way, and for how long

KSh 300

Power of Attorney

Appoint someone to act for you, generally or for specific matters.

What you'll need
  • Your details and ID number
  • Your attorney’s details and ID number
  • Exactly which powers, and for how long

KSh 150

Parental Consent for Child Travel

Consent for a minor to travel with one parent, a relative or a group.

What you'll need
  • Parents’ names, ID numbers and contacts
  • Child’s name, date of birth and passport number
  • Who the child travels with, where and when

KSh 200

Demand Letter

A firm, formal demand for money owed or an obligation that hasn’t been met.

What you'll need
  • Who owes and their address
  • What is owed and why
  • Deadline and how to pay
